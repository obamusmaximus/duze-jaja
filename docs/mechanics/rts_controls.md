# RTS controls: picking, selection, control groups, order clicks

The local player's RTS-view controls (the left-click scheme in `GAME_DESIGN.md`, "Controls"). View side: they change the game only by sending orders (`orders.md`). Tunables: `PlayerControlStats` (`tuning/player_control_default.tres`, Controls tab).

**Scripts:** `picking/ground_pick.gd`, `flyer_pick.gd`; `player/rts_player.gd` (+ `rts_player.tscn`), `player/pointer_input.gd`; `selection/selection.gd`, `selection_control.gd`, `control_groups.gd`, `selection_box.gd`; `orders/order_control.gd`, `order_marker.gd`. Level wiring: `levels/sandbox/sandbox.gd`.

## How it works
- **Ground picking (`GroundPick`, static):** `ground_point(viewport, screen_point)` casts from the viewport's active camera on layer 1 and returns the hit or `GroundPick.NONE` (test `is_finite()`); `object_under()` returns the unit or building (layers 2, 3) under the point, unless ground is in front of it. In a level with water, a ground point below sea level is replaced by the point on the sea's surface (the sea has no collision, so the ray would otherwise land on the seabed, off from the click). Call from `_physics_process`. Everything that clicks the world goes through it.
- **Picking flyers (`FlyerPick`, static):** on screen, not by physics: a flyer's body or its ground plate, each a projected circle at least `flyer_pick_min_px` wide, hidden if the ground is in front. `object_under` takes a flyer body first, then whatever the ray hits, then a flyer plate (so a ground unit standing on a plate wins). A box takes a flyer by its body or its ground point.
- **`RtsPlayer`** (one per local player, `setup(team, selection, issuer, camera_rig)`) wires the parts. `PointerInput` reports gestures decided on release: a left press moved past `drag_threshold_px` is a box, otherwise a click (with the OS double-click flag); a right press that didn't move that far is a click (farther was a camera drag pan). `RtsPlayer` queues gestures and resolves them on the next physics tick, where picking is safe.
- **What a left click does:** own unit → select it (unless attack-move is armed); enemy → inspect (`Selection.inspect`: the selection empties and the selection module shows the enemy as one unit with its health, no hold contents; any new own selection ends it; probe event `inspect`). Left click on empty ground clears the selection. **Right click orders** (StarCraft scheme): enemy → attack, ground → move (attack-move when armed); with no selection it only cancels attack-move. An armed attack-move takes the next left click. A right press that moved past the threshold is a camera drag pan, not an order.
- **`Selection`** (owned by the level) is the player's list of own units, signal `changed`; it shows and hides the rings and drops units that leave the match. With several types it keeps a **main type** (`main_type`, `set_main_type`, `main_units`, signal `main_changed`): kept while selected, else the most numerous type. The HUD's cards pick it; F (`unit_ability`) sends `Order.ability` for `main_units()` (`OrderControl`). `cycle_main_type` (backquote, since Tab is the keybind page) steps to the next type (`SelectionControl.cycle_main_type`).
- **`SelectionControl`:** click replaces the selection; `select_add` (Shift) toggles a clicked unit or adds a box; double-click selects every own unit of the type on screen. A box projects unit centres (and flyers' ground points) to the screen (`FlyerPick.in_box`), no physics, own team only. Probe: `selected` ids; events `select`, `box_select`, `inspect`.
- **`ControlGroups`:** `control_group_set` (Ctrl or Cmd) + `control_group_<n>` stores the selection; the number recalls it; the same number again within `group_double_tap_seconds` (real time) also calls `CameraRig.focus_on` with the group's centre. Events `group_set`, `group_recall`.
- **`OrderControl`:** builds orders from the selection and sends them to the `CommandIssuer`. `order_queue` (Alt) held queues a waypoint; `order_attack_move` (A) arms attack-move for the next left click (orders are right clicks); `order_stop` (S) stops; `unit_ability` (F) calls `use_ability`: an untargeted ability fires at once, a targeted one (`units.md`, "Abilities") is armed if any main-type unit is ready. Armed: cross cursor, the next left click aims it (a unit or a ground point; a click it can't take leaves it armed); right click, F again, A, S or a selection or main-type change cancels. The selection panel's ability button does the same as F, through `HudCommands` (`RtsPlayer.bind_hud`). Feedback: the cursor (move over ground, cross over enemies or while armed) and an `OrderMarker` ring where an order landed (on the ground: under a flyer that was attacked), for `order_marker_seconds` (real time).
- **Strategy views (10c-4 to 10c-6):** `views/strategy/`. The RTS player is off in the bird's-eye view and on the table, so `StrategyControl` takes the mouse there (not during a flight). Left click: with a marker armed (`StrategyTools`, set by the commands panel buttons) put it at the ground point under the cursor (`StrategyProjector.ground_at`: a ray marched against the height field, scaled onto the table on the war table), else pick the nearest own figure (`StrategyFigures`: squad, unit or carryall, within `pick_radius_px`) and select its unit ids through `Selection`; Shift adds; empty ground clears; an enemy figure of one unit inspects it as in the RTS view (a squad figure only raises the `inspect` event); own mothership raises `pick_mothership` (nothing to select yet). Right click without a drag: cancels an armed marker, else clears a marker within `marker_clear_px`, else move (ground) or attack (enemy figure) for the selection, Alt queues. Picking is in screen space, no physics. `StrategyOverlay` (canvas layer 6, between blips and HUD) draws the team's markers (a glyph per kind and its name), a ghost of the armed marker at the cursor, and the strategy outline: corner brackets round every selected figure (orange) and the hovered one (off-white), a squad's bounds being its members' bounds. Tunables: `StrategyControlStats` (Controls tab). Markers are not drawn in the RTS view.
- **Health bars** (`HealthBars`, `combat.md`) are drawn in `RtsPlayer`'s `BarLayer` and hide when the player is switched off (the strategy views).
- **Actions:** `select` (LMB), `order_click` (RMB), `select_add` (Shift), `control_group_set` (Ctrl, Cmd), `control_group_1..6` (number row; 0, 7, 8, 9 are free for debug keys), `order_attack_move` (A), `order_stop` (S), `order_queue` (Alt).

## Known issues
- Keys for attack-move, stop and the modifiers were picked without checking the RA3 / C&C3 manuals.
- Ctrl+number can be taken by macOS (Mission Control desktop shortcuts); Cmd+number works too.

- **Keybind page:** Tab opens a reference of every key (`ui.md`, `heightmap.md`); keep its rows in `ui/keybinds/keybind_page.gd` in step with the Input Map.

- **Carryalls:** a move click with only loaded carryalls selected gives a `deliver` order (`carryall.md`).
