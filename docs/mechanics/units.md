# Units and teams

Units as simulation objects: identity, parts, movement. Design: `GAME_DESIGN.md` ("Views", "Multiplayer and simulation"). Tunables: `UnitStats` (`tuning/unit_default.tres`, Units tab). Orders: `orders.md`.

**Scripts:** `debug/benchmark/benchmark.gd` (standing benchmark), `units/unit.gd` (+ `unit.tscn`), `unit_registry.gd`, `unit_brain.gd`, `order_brain.gd`, `unit_look.gd`, `flight_autopilot.gd`, `attack_run.gd`, `pilot_brain.gd`, `pilot_seats.gd` (+ `orders/pilot_input.gd`). Movement, navigator and the spatial hash: `pathing.md`. Health, weapon, targeting: `combat.md`.

## How it works
- **`Unit`** (`CharacterBody3D`, layer 2, masks 1 and 3: units never collide with each other, see `pathing.md`) only holds its parts (look, movement, navigator, order queue, brain, targeting, weapon, health) and identity: `id`, `team`, `stats`, `alive`. It has no `_physics_process`: the registry calls `think(delta)` (targeting, `movement.decide`, weapon) and `movement.integrate`. A **unit type** is a `UnitStats` `.tres`: units sharing one are the same type. Groups: `units`, `team_<n>`. Signals: `died`, `selected_changed`. `center()` is the body's middle (screen projection, later aiming).
- **`UnitRegistry`** (the level's `Units` node) is the parent of every unit. It gives each a stable **unit id** (from 1, never reused in a match), looks living units up by id (`get_unit`) and by place (`units_near`), and hands units the match services (`map`, `paths`, `shots`). The level calls `setup(map, paths, shots)` once the map is built, which registers the units placed as its children in child order; spawners use `add(unit)` with `team` set first. Registering calls `Unit.setup(id, registry)`, which wires the parts.
- **The tick:** `UnitRegistry._physics_process` is the one loop. Half the units think each tick (by id parity) with twice the delta; every unit integrates (turns and moves by what it last decided) every tick, so physics interpolation stays smooth. `Unit.full_rate` makes a unit think every tick (the inhabited unit). Timers and cooldowns just see the doubled delta.
- **Kinematic movement:** ground units are moved by setting their position: height from `MapGrid.surface_height` (blended between cell centres), a move into a blocked cell is refused and slides along it, and a unit standing in a cell that became blocked is moved to the nearest walkable one on its next rest check. The `CharacterBody3D` and its shape stay for queries only. `UnitMovement.physical = true` brings back `move_and_slide` with gravity for direct control (not wired to anything yet).
- **Ids, not nodes:** `Selection` and `ControlGroups` hold unit ids (`Selection.units` resolves them); `UnitTargeting.in_range`/`in_sight`, `Weapon.target` and the attack-move engaged target store ids behind properties that resolve through `Unit.registry`.
- **Brain:** `UnitMovement` asks its **brain** each tick for a velocity (`wanted_velocity(delta)`) and, while standing, a facing (`wanted_facing()`). `OrderBrain` (behaviour by order, `combat.md`) drives units by default; `set_brain()` swaps in another (`PilotBrain` while inhabited).
- **Inhabiting** (the simulation side; the first-person view is in `cockpit.md`):
  - **Seats:** `PilotSeats` (`CommandIssuer.seats`) holds the unit each team's player inhabits, one each (a team has one player for now). Entered and left only by order (`orders.md`). `can_inhabit` is the one rule for which units: living flyers that aren't carryalls; widen it there for ground units and carryalls.
  - **Entering** swaps in a `PilotBrain`, clears the order queue, path, weapon target and idle orbit, and sets `full_rate`. The unit doesn't fire while inhabited (first-person weapons aren't built); enemies still target it.
  - **Ending:** by order, or by itself when the unit dies or leaves the world alive. Signals `inhabited(team, unit)` and `released(team, unit, reason: LEFT, DIED, REMOVED)`; the unit is still readable during `released`, so a view can take its position. The order brain gets the unit back idle: a hover type stays, a fixed wing circles to its left from where it is. Both hand-overs keep the autopilot's velocity.
  - **Placeholder flying** (until the flight model): the autopilot flies toward `PilotInput.aim` flattened onto the air layer and keeps its own height. Fixed wing: top speed, `flight_min_speed` with the air brake, straight on with no aim. Hover: flies at the aim, the brake stops it and turns it on the spot, hovers with no aim. Stick and ability do nothing yet.
- **Look** (`UnitLook`, visual only): the type's `model` (UnitStats "Look") over a flat team-colour base plate, or with no model a placeholder hull cylinder on the plate with a livery nose showing the facing (colours from `WorldMaterials`); plus the selection ring. Models: `props.md`. Built from `radius`, `height` and rebuilt on `changed`, as is the collision shape (local to each unit).
- **Sandbox:** six team 0 units and four team 1 units under `Units`, plus a Hornet (flyer) per team.
- **Flyers** (`UnitStats` "Flight") move on the air layer (`pathing.md`) and are flown by **`FlightAutopilot`** (`units/flight_autopilot.gd`, on `UnitMovement.autopilot`): the cheap, always-safe model of RTS and AI flight. `UnitMovement` gives it each decision (`command`: wanted velocity, facing, separation push) and steps it every tick. It keeps a real `velocity` (three axes) and `orientation()` (yaw is the node's, plus `pitch` and `bank`), so the flight model of `docs/plans/flight.md` step 3 can take over and hand back without a jump. The node and its collision shape only yaw; `UnitLook.set_attitude` tilts the airframe.
  - **Hover** (`flight_style`; Hornet, VTOL, carryall): turns at `turn_rate`, gains and loses speed at `flight_acceleration` along where it faces, stops and hovers. The navigator brakes it early enough. It leans into acceleration.
  - **Fixed wing** (fighter): flies only along its nose, between `flight_min_speed` and `speed`, so it turns in arcs. A move arrives on passing within `flight_arrive_radius`, then it **orbits** that point (`flight_orbit_radius`, never tighter than it can turn; always anticlockwise, so planes never meet head on). With no order and no point (spawn, stop) it circles to its left. A point inside its tightest turn is not chased: it flies on until it can come round (`approach`). It has no stuck or crowd arrival.
  - **Height:** `max(ground + flight_min_clearance, flight_height + flight_terrain_follow × ground)` (ground at least sea level), reached at `flight_climb_rate`. It looks `flight_look_ahead` seconds along its course (curved while turning): each sample asks for its height less what can still be climbed on the way, so the climb starts just in time. For ground it couldn't foresee (a turn into a steep face): an emergency pull-up past the climb rate, hover types slow until high enough, and a hard floor just above the ground. `altitude_override` (pads, landings) replaces all of this.
  - **Separation** only between flyers within `flight_separation_height` of each other.
- **Reading height** (`UnitLook`, look only): the team plate and ring stay on the ground beneath, tilted to the slope there and raised by `sea_mark_lift` over open sea so waves don't cover them. A round shadow (`WorldMaterials.blob_shadow`, `shadow_scale`, `shadow_opacity`) lies straight below, and a team-colour line (`height_line_width`) runs from plate to body, hidden when nearly landed. `Unit.ground_point()` and `hover_height` give that spot to views (picking, order markers).
- **Test flyers:** `tuning/unit_fighter.tres` (fixed wing, fast, fragile, anti-air) and `unit_vtol.tres` (hover, slower, tougher), placed on the atoll (`battlefield.md`), with their own icons. The fighter has its own gun (`tuning/weapon_fighter.tres`: longer reach, quick light shots) so a pass lands several hits.
- **Anti-air:** `UnitStats.anti_air` (Combat). `Unit.can_attack(other)` is the one rule: an enemy, and in the air only if anti-air. Targeting, attack orders (refused for ground units, the rest of a selection still goes) and the attack brain all use it. Units that aren't anti-air never pick a flyer. (In first person, most weapons will hit air; not built.)

- **Cargo:** `UnitStats` cargo class, slots and optional hold; `Unit.cargo`. See `cargo.md`.

## Known issues
- Placeholder flying: an inhabited fixed wing aimed off the map is pinned against the edge until the aim turns back in.
- `sight_range` is only a notice range for attack-move; there is no fog of war.
- Flyers collide with nothing (mask 0; they are kinematic now too), so they pass over walls, buildings and the debug wall. The air layer holds no obstacles: props and buildings aren't in the heights the autopilot reads.
