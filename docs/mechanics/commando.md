# Commando (jetpack infantry)

The first special unit for players (`docs/plans/match_flow.md` step 12): a jetpack soldier, ported from the jetpack loadout of the sibling FPS `../first` (its `docs/mechanics/jet.md`, `jet_tracking.md`, `seeker_shotgun.md`). A ground unit in the RTS view that hovers, surges and afterburns; inhabited (J), a first-person shooter with the full kit. Tunables: `JetpackStats` (`tuning/jetpack_default.tres`), `SeekerShotgunStats`, `TrackingStats`, `JetHudStats`, all on the tuning panel's Commando tab; the type is `tuning/unit_commando.tres` (`UnitStats.jetpack`), its RTS gun `weapon_commando.tres` (`damage_commando.tres`), its pellets `weapon_seeker.tres` (`damage_seeker.tres`).

**Scripts:** `units/jetpack.gd` (`Jetpack`, RTS), `units/jetpack_model.gd` (`JetpackModel`, first person), `units/pilot_flight.gd` (jetpack branch), `combat/jet_tracking.gd`, `combat/seeker_shotgun.gd`, `combat/seeker_pellets.gd`, `combat/pellet_tracers.gd` (view); HUD parts `views/cockpit/hud/jet_part.gd`, `jet_crosshair.gd`, `jet_scanner.gd`, `jet_tracks.gd`, `jet_status.gd`; `views/cockpit/pilot_controls.gd` and `cockpit_view.gd` (jetpack mode). Model: Kenney Space Kit `astronautB`; icon `ui/icons/units/commando.svg`.

## How it works
- **A ground unit that hovers.** `is_flying()` is false: it paths on the map grid, is refused other land masses, is hit by ground weapons and carried as infantry. `Unit.hovers()` is true: `UnitMovement` keeps its body `rts_hover_height` (a grunt's height) above the ground, and `UnitLook` gives it a flyer's ground plate, shadow and height line.
- **RTS movement (`Jetpack`, stepped in `Unit.think` after the movement decided):** any move is a surge at the type's `speed`. Moving within `rts_straight_deg` of the way a run began for `rts_afterburner_delay` lights the **afterburner**: `boost` rises at `rts_afterburner_accel` toward `rts_afterburner_speed`, applied by `UnitMovement` only while the brain asks for cruising speed (not while braking to arrive). A sharper turn or stopping ends it. Standing is the hover. No fuel. The body leans forward with speed (`rts_lean_deg`, look only).
- **RTS weapon:** an instant shot (`weapon_commando`; no pellets drawn, the usual tracer) at one target. Its damage profile: instakill on infantry, two hits on the rover (`LIGHT_VEHICLE` armor, its own type for this), `vs_explorer` on Explorers, the fallback on everything else. **Under the afterburner it can't fire** (`Unit.think` only cools the weapon).
- **Inhabiting:** `PilotSeats.can_inhabit` takes any living jetpack type. `UnitMovement.start_flight` gives it a `PilotFlight` running a `JetpackModel` (no autopilot); leaving resets the RTS jetpack and settles it back to its hover over the nearest walkable cell (`SETTLE_RINGS`).
- **First person (`JetpackModel`, from `../first`'s `JetMotor`):** the view is the mouse's own (`PilotControls` in jetpack mode, pitch within `view_pitch_limit_deg`); the body turns with it. On the ground it walks (`walk_speed`, `walk_accel`) and jumps (Space, `jump_speed`); `ground_snap` keeps it on ground that drops away. The jet key (`pilot_jet`, Shift) toggles **hover** on a press shorter than `surge_hold_time` (low gravity, vertical damping, WASD strafing at `hover_strafe_speed`); held longer with a movement key it **surges** along the keys relative to the view, pitch included (`surge_speed`, `surge_steer_accel`), and after `afterburner_delay` of forward-only surging the **afterburner** accelerates against a squared drag toward `afterburner_speed`. Its momentum carries into the hover until slowed to the strafe speed. Free flight with hover off: gravity, `air_accel`, `air_drag`. It enters still hovering. No fuel, no wall run.
- **Contact (`PilotFlight._jetpack_contact`):** the ground and sea (`AirLayer.ground_height`) are its floor; buildings (Building layer, a ray) stop it and it slides along them. It never crashes.
- **Tracking (`JetTracking`):** the scanner is a pyramid along the view, `scanner_half_width_deg` by `scanner_half_height_deg`, out to `scanner_range`. An enemy in it and in sight (no World or Building between) for `scanner_pickup_time` is tracked for `track_time`, refreshed while in it, up to `max_tracks`. While the afterburner burns, every enemy in sight within `scout_range` is tracked at once for `scout_track_time`. Tracks are unit ids, in pickup order.
- **Seeker shotgun (`SeekerShotgun`, `SeekerPellets`):** each press of fire (`refire_time` apart; none under the afterburner) sends `pellet_count` pellets from `muzzle_offset` in a `spread_degrees` cone at the crosshair's point (within `aim_range`). Tracks are dealt to the pellets newest pickup first, round again. A pellet flies straight for `homing_delay`, then turns at `turn_rate`; one whose enemy is inside its turning circle gives up and flies on (`brake_into_turns` brakes instead). A sphere cast stops it on the ground, a building or a unit; an enemy takes `weapon_seeker`'s damage (`Shots.strike`), and the sea stops it. Sound and sparks come from `Shots`; the weapon has `muzzle_flash` off.
- **HUD** (parts of the `HelmetHud`, shown only for a jetpack type; `HelmetPart.shows`): a crosshair ring at the centre (dimmed under the afterburner), the scanner frame projected through the camera with brighter corner marks, a box on each track sized in world metres with a segmented health bar (`health_segment_hp`), blinking near its end, and under them H and S boxes (filled while on; S's outline runs clockwise while the key is held toward a surge), speed in km/h and an orange A/B. Style pass: thin `UiTokens.HUD_LINE` strokes with the dark edge, like the flyers' helmet HUD; no colours in stats. Tracers (`PelletTracers`, drawn by `CockpitView`) are HUD green warming to white at the head.
- **Test units:** `BattlefieldStats.test_commandos` per team, in a row inland of their airfield.

## Settled
- Headless on the atoll: hovers 1.8 m up; a straight move surges, then afterburns at 26 m/s after 2 s; stops to the hover. Inhabited: lands, surges at 50 m/s, afterburns; a trooper and a rover in the scanner are both tracked within 2.5 s and the rover takes pellet hits.

## Known issues
- F does nothing yet (no ability). The fuel tank, the micro weapons and bombardment from `../first` aren't ported.
- No jet sounds (thrust roar, hover hum): the cockpit's engine loop plays instead. No FOV kick on a surge.
- In first person it stands on the sea and passes through props and walls on the World layer (only buildings stop it).
- Leaving it on another land mass leaves it there (RTS orders to its home island are refused); leaving it far out at sea keeps it there.
- Pellets in flight vanish when the seat ends. Pellet spread uses `randf()` (not deterministic yet).
- Enemies tracked behind the camera have no marker; no sign of a pickup in progress.
