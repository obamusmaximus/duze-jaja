# Cockpit (first-person view)

The view of the unit the player inhabits. Design: `GAME_DESIGN.md` ("Flight", "Views"); build order: `docs/plans/flight.md`. The simulation side (seats, the control stream, who can be inhabited) is in `units.md`, "Inhabiting". Tunables: `CockpitStats` (`tuning/cockpit_default.tres`, Camera tab), `HelmetHudStats` (`tuning/helmet_hud_default.tres`, Flight tab), `FlightSoundStats` (`tuning/flight_sound_default.tres`, Audio tab; the three loops' loudness is on their cues) and each type's `UnitStats.cockpit_offset`. The two control types, jet and hover, are in `flight_model.md`. A jetpack type (the commando) is first person on foot instead: its controls, camera and HUD parts are in `commando.md`.

**Scripts:** `views/cockpit/cockpit_view.gd`, `pilot_controls.gd`, `cockpit_feel.gd`, `cockpit_sound.gd`; `views/cockpit/hud/` (`helmet_weapon.gd`, `helmet_hud.gd`, `helmet_part.gd`, `helmet_ladder.gd`, `helmet_markers.gd`, `helmet_tapes.gd`, `helmet_warnings.gd`, `flight_warnings.gd`); `views/view_switcher.gd` (`hand_over`, `take_back`), `views/view_transition.gd`; `units/unit_look.gd` (cockpit mount, hidden airframe). Built by `AtollLevel._build_war_table` (atoll only).

## How it works
- **View only.** `CockpitView` changes the game only through orders (`inhabit`, `leave`) and `CommandIssuer.pilot()`.
- **J (`inhabit`)** takes the one selected unit from the RTS or bird's-eye view, or leaves. It does nothing during a V flight. More or fewer than one selected unit, or a refused order, plays the error cue.
- **Everything follows the seat signals,** so J, death and removal end the same way, and an order issued by anything else opens the view too. States: off, entering, in, leaving. J while leaving does nothing.
- **The switch** is its own `ViewTransition`, from an **anchor camera** (a still copy of whatever is on screen, with its environment) to the moving cockpit camera, or on the way out to the RTS camera. So it never snaps, from the bird's-eye view or when interrupted. Out lands in the RTS view centred on the ground under the unit, keeping the RTS yaw.
- **`ViewSwitcher.hand_over()`** turns every rig and `RtsPlayer` off, reports the view as RTS (the strategy controls close), ignores V and B until `take_back()`, and keeps the far sea up for the horizon. Camera, selection, control groups, orders and health bars are off with it; half-made gestures and an armed attack-move are dropped.
- **Aim correction (`aim_correction`):** the aim works as a mouse stick that springs back: every tick it eases toward its **centre** (`FlightSteering.neutral_aim`), the aim that asks for nothing, so turns end and the wings level without centring by hand. The centre is the nose less the trim lead: the nose itself at cruise, a little under it when slow (aiming at the bare nose would creep upward). It levels the wings, not the climb: a climb or dive is held. Paused while a jet's stick flies; free look uses its own faster rate.
- **Camera:** at the unit's **cockpit mount** (`Unit.cockpit_mount()`, a node on the airframe at `cockpit_offset`), posed each frame from its interpolated transform, so it pitches and banks with the plane. The `Sfx` listener moves onto it. Its own airframe, plate, ring and height line are hidden from `hide_airframe_blend` on; the ground shadow stays as a height cue.
- **Feel (`CockpitFeel`):** the field of view widens with speed (`speed_fov_*`), and the camera's angle shakes at high speed and buffets from `shake_stall_from` of the stall angle into the stall. Speeds here are shares of the type's level top speed, so one setting fits both types. The buffet reads `FlightModel.stall_load()` (share of the stall angle used; 0 while hovering).
- **Sound (`CockpitSound`):** an engine loop (pitch by speed, higher in a hover) and a wind loop (loudness and pitch by speed, more in hard turns and on the air brake), fading with the switch and after the flight ends, and paused with the game. The **stall warning** beeps from `warning_load`, faster and higher once stalled. One engine sound for both types.
- **Helmet HUD (`HelmetHud`):** thin lines in `UiTokens.HUD_LINE` with a dark edge, warnings in `HUD_WARNING`, drawn by `_draw` on its own canvas layer. It fades in over the end of the flight in and is gone at once on leaving. The root reads the flight once a frame (nose from the interpolated mount, velocity blended between ticks); its parts only draw. `Hud.Mode.FLIGHT` still shows the empty `ui/hud/layouts/flight.tres`: card modules don't suit these marks.
  - **On the world** (projected through the showing camera, so right in free look too): horizon, pitch ladder (the rungs near the nose, dashed below the horizon, ticks toward it), **boresight** (the nose, a flat W), **flight path marker** (held crossed out at the screen edge when out of view, gone below `path_min_speed`), mouse-aim ring (the virtual joystick) with a hairline to it from the boresight (mid-screen unless the head is turned; `HUD_REFERENCE`, left out when the two overlap).
  - **With the helmet** (fixed on screen): speed tape in km/h with the stall speed marked, altitude tape in metres above the sea with `GND` (airframe's underside to ground or sea) below, compass heading tape (north is -z), angle-of-attack gauge, notes for air brake, hover and transition. In a hover the gauge, stall mark, stall limit bar and pull-up are off, and the flight path marker is left out when out of view.
- **Reading a stall coming:** the gauge shows the share of the stall angle in use (`stall_load()`) with the warning and stall marked and a **trend caret** (`trend_time` ahead). From `limit_from` a **stall limit bar** stands one stall angle off the flight path marker: the boresight reaching it is the stall. Gauge, bar and a STALL banner turn orange with the warning; the banner flashes once stalled. HUD and beep share one rule (`FlightWarnings.stall`, `FlightSoundStats.warning_load`).
- **Pull up (`FlightWarnings.ground`):** the velocity, held straight, is sampled against ground and sea for `pull_up_time` seconds, plus up to `pull_up_dive_time` the steeper the dive. It warns only while closing on the ground, so low level flight is quiet.
- **Controls (`PilotControls`):** the mouse is captured (freed while paused; `CameraInput` leaves a captured mouse alone) and its screen motion turns a world-direction **aim**, kept within `aim_max_off_nose` of the nose's heading and `aim_pitch_limit` of level. `aim_roll_follow` sets how far the mouse's directions roll with the plane (at 0, up is the sky). While the **stick** is held the aim rides the flight path (the nose in a hover), so letting go holds that course (`aim_follows_stick`); `stick_invert_pitch` swaps W and S. While **free look** is held (right mouse, C or middle mouse; both control types) the mouse turns the head instead, and the aim eases back to centre (`free_look_aim_return`), so the craft stops turning; on release the head eases back. In a hover WASD never moves the aim. A `PilotInput` goes out every physics tick.
- **Ability readout** (`HelmetAbility`, `views/cockpit/hud/helmet_ability.gd`): under the altitude tape, key and name, a bar and the `AbilityStatus` line (`units.md`, "Abilities"); dimmed while it can't be used; nothing for a type without an ability.
- **Weapon** (rules in `combat.md`): `PilotControls` sends the trigger, the lock key and the **view** (where the head looks, shake left out). `HelmetWeapon` draws a crosshair at the view (always for a turret type, else while the head is turned, to pick a lock) with the reload line under it, and a box with the distance on the locked target, orange while a shot would bend onto it, held at the screen edge when out of view.
- **Actions:** `pilot_fire` (left mouse), `pilot_lock` (R), `inhabit` (J), `free_look` (right mouse, C, middle mouse), `unit_ability` (F: `units.md`, "Abilities"). WASD (`pilot_pitch_up/down`, `pilot_roll_left/right`) is a jet's stick, and a hover's forward, back and strafe; `pilot_up` (Space) and `pilot_down` (Shift) are hover only; `air_brake` (B, shared with the war table's key, ignored here) is jet only. `stick_invert_pitch` and `aim_follows_stick` apply to the jet only. How they fly: `flight_model.md`.
- **Autopilot (H):** flies to the nearest airfield and lands while you stay seated; H again takes the controls back (`airfield.md`). A carryall flies to a mothership pad. F with a deploy ability also puts the unit on the autopilot (`carryall.md`, `explorer.md`); on the autopilot only the ability key is still sent.

## Known issues
- There is no cockpit interior: the view is bare.
- Pull up is silent, and doesn't see buildings, props or other flyers.
- The ladder and horizon can cross the tapes (no clipping around them).
- The feel and sound have not had the feel pass yet; the loops are synthesized placeholders.
- A full-stick pull goes from the warning to the stall in a fraction of a second; lower `warning_load` for more notice. A VTOL pulled hard just out of the hover stalls with next to none.
- A flyer aimed off the map is pinned against the edge (`flight_model.md`).
- Leaving lands on the RTS rig's old ground height until its ground follow catches up (a small settle).
- Frame time in the cockpit isn't measured on the target machine; the view to the horizon draws far more than the RTS view's patch of ground.
